## 12. Tone / style notes for the Harvard-CV draft

- **Source PDF style** — design-heavy, two-column CakeResume template, photo + colour, Vietnamese-first content with English certification names.
- **Harvard style is the opposite** — single column, no photo, no colour, conservative serif font (typically Times or Computer Modern), strict reverse-chronological sections, hard 1-page (occasionally 2-page) limit, action-verb bullet points beginning with strong verbs, measurable outcomes.
- **What carries over** — the technical-skills inventory, the certifications block (Harvard CVs do include cert lists for technical roles), the project links, the work-history bullets.
- **What gets cut** — the motto/personal-philosophy paragraph (Harvard CVs don't carry personal statements; only the professional Summary at top, if any), the photo, the colour, the design columns.
- **What gets added** — measurable impact (the Chatty M-series has real numbers: −5pp churn, $9K MRR retained, ~Day 2–3 → ~60 min setup, +3pp trial-to-paid). Quantified outcomes are the Harvard-style signal.
